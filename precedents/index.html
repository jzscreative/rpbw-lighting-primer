Renzo Piano for Lighting Designers/Precedent gallery

Lighting precedent gallery

Night and dusk lighting precedents for four themes: glass-floored elevated walkways and the plazas beneath them; lit floating roof planes; libraries; auditoria. Renzo Piano Building Workshop projects first, then other architects, mostly since 2015. 821 images (90 night, 259 dusk, 173 day, 261 interiors under electric light, 38 interiors in daylight). Photographs are third-party, collected for study; each card credits the photographer where known and links to its source. Click an image to view it larger, then use the arrow keys (or swipe) to step through the filtered set; Esc closes.
